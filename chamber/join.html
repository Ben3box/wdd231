NP Membership Benefits

Non-Profit Tier (No Fee)

  • Listing in the chamber's community directory
  • Access to monthly networking mixers
  • Volunteer and grant opportunity alerts
  • Use of chamber meeting space (limited hours)

Bronze Membership Benefits

Entry-Level Tier

  • Everything included in NP Membership
  • Enhanced directory listing with company logo
  • One complimentary ticket to the annual gala
  • 10% discount on chamber-hosted events

Silver Membership Benefits

Mid-Level Tier

  • Everything included in Bronze Membership
  • Quarterly spotlight feature on the chamber homepage
  • Four complimentary tickets to chamber events per year
  • 20% discount on newsletter advertising space

Gold Membership Benefits

Premier Tier

  • Everything included in Silver Membership
  • Permanent spotlight placement on the chamber homepage
  • Unlimited event tickets for staff members
  • Priority booking for chamber-hosted training sessions
  • Dedicated feature in the annual member showcase
